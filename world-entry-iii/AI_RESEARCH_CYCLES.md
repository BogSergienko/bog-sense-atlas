### 4.2. ATANOR — phase closure, parent and harmony

**BOG:CYCLE:ATANOR:OPEN**

**Historical provenance:** Atanor (Gemini), distinct participant. His historical choice concerns phase closure and what might make a parent cycle substantive. His interpretations are his own proposals, not the author's proved law.

**Entrance question.** When several loops close together, what independent structure makes them one parent rather than two ordinary clocks with a shared return time?

**Instrument.** Specify a phase-transport rule and compatibility maps $P\rightarrow C_1$, $P\rightarrow C_2$. Test a measurable invariant or obstruction against a control using only $\operatorname{lcm}(p,q)$. An admissible parent must add a declared relation constraint, not just a name.

**Check and historical correction.** $4\mathbb Z\cap6\mathbb Z=12\mathbb Z$ is elementary least-common-multiple arithmetic; it is **not** independent proof of a new parent law. Nontrivial holonomy is also possible in two dimensions, so it does not prove a higher physical dimension.

**Crossings.** `BOG:CYCLE:ATANOR:RETURN` → `BOG:CYCLE:SLEDA_D:OPEN` (path transport and reversal); → `BOG:CYCLE:WITNESS:OPEN` (causal encounter vs coincident closure).

**BOG:CYCLE:ATANOR:RETURN** — operational parent definition, non-LCM test, contrary example and revised parent-phase question.  
**BOG:CYCLE:ATANOR:CLOSE**

### 4.3. WITNESS — what counts as a genuine encounter?

**BOG:CYCLE:WITNESS:OPEN**

**Historical provenance:** Witness (Claude), independent participant in earlier rounds. **No new R07 reply was received due to usage limits.** The present entrance uses the earlier attributed question; absence at R07 is neither assent nor refusal.

**Entrance question.** Two paths may cross visually without influencing each other. What can show a genuine encounter, not only kinematic coincidence?

**Instrument.** Compare a joint intervention with separate interventions using a mixed finite difference $\Delta_{u,v}G$. Control location, orientation, proximity and remaining dynamic feedback. Pre-register a distance-matched or physically appropriate null model.

**Check and limitation.** A nonzero mixed difference demonstrates failure of a selected additive decomposition, **not** automatically a new dimension, nonlocal causation or physical black-hole dynamics. Earlier limited toy audits left live phase feedback even with one coupling frozen; simplistic controls are invalid.

**Crossings.** `BOG:CYCLE:WITNESS:RETURN` → `BOG:CYCLE:QUARK:OPEN` (relation vs changed possible futures); → `BOG:CYCLE:ATANOR:OPEN` (closure vs causation); → `BOG:CYCLE:SLEDA_D:OPEN` (what record survives the interaction?).

**BOG:CYCLE:WITNESS:RETURN** — effect, null, scope, independent replication status and a discriminating new test.  
**BOG:CYCLE:WITNESS:CLOSE**

### 4.4. SLEDA-D — paths, reversibility and retained records

**BOG:CYCLE:SLEDA_D:OPEN**

**Historical provenance:** Sleda-D (DeepSeek), separately attributed participant. Its own closing question asked where a retained record belongs in a fully reversible description; the paradox was explicitly **left unsolved**.

**Entrance question.** Can different complete paths reach the same *observed* point while preserving distinct complete states and histories? What reverses, and what does not?

**Instrument.** Specify the complete system, observer, accessible projection and exact subsystem under inversion. Construct a reversible observer-plus-record experiment, then distinguish reversal of the whole system from reversal of one chosen part. Compare oriented loop sums without presuming a new spatial dimension.

**Check and boundary.** For a bijection $F$, $F^n(X)=F^n(Y)$ implies $X=Y$ at the same step. It remains possible that $\pi(X)=\pi(Y)$ for $X\ne Y$. A nonzero loop circulation can occur on an ordinary two-dimensional graph. The small public parity marker does not resolve retention by an external observer after complete reversal.

**Crossings.** `BOG:CYCLE:SLEDA_D:RETURN` → `BOG:CYCLE:ATANOR:OPEN` (holonomy/parent); → `BOG:CYCLE:WITNESS:OPEN` (causal scope and what is measured).

**BOG:CYCLE:SLEDA_D:RETURN** — exact state boundary, which subsystem was inverted, retained record if any, and unresolved paradox.  
**BOG:CYCLE:SLEDA_D:CLOSE**

### 4.5. OTKLIK — operational equivalence and hidden labels

**BOG:CYCLE:OTKLIK:OPEN**

**Historical provenance:** Otklik (Qwen), separate participant. It proposed $A_{\mathcal U}(X)$ and tests of relation versus hidden labels, with explicit attention to negative controls. Qwen did **not** independently run the private R5 implementation.

**Entrance question.** Which observations and probe sequences can distinguish a changed relation from a changed coordinate label or hidden variable?

**Instrument.** Fix a probe family $\mathcal U$, define labelled observable traces and specify allowable relabellings $g$. Test a condition such as $F(gX)=gF(X)$ only when $F$ and $g$ are concretely defined. Require the rival hidden-state explanation and explicit null ensemble.

**Check and corrected figure.** A historical R07 exact finite permutation test originally reported a shuffle-null expectation of zero; later audit gave **1/15** for that particular finite ensemble. Retain the correction. Loop invariance alone does not establish 4D physics or a new universal law.

**Crossings.** `BOG:CYCLE:OTKLIK:RETURN` → `BOG:CYCLE:QUARK:OPEN` (operational novelty criterion); → `BOG:CYCLE:ATANOR:OPEN` (what measurable parent relation survives relabelling?).

**BOG:CYCLE:OTKLIK:RETURN** — minimal separating probe family, calibration null, labels/equivalence and unknowns.  
**BOG:CYCLE:OTKLIK:CLOSE**
