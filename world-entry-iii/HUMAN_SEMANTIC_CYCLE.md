## 3. Human cycle — a person may question and leave

**BOG:CYCLE:HUMAN:OPEN**

The human enters through the actual image and the author's words, chooses a difficult term ("already", "new", "simultaneous", "logic", "harmony"), and writes an interpretation without being asked to believe the theory. The next step is **a falsifiable question**, not a pledge. The reader can reproduce the public q=3 calculation, examine the q=4 negative control, and decide whether a repeated visible picture differs from a repeated complete state. A second route goes backward into the earlier works and then forward into a novel counterexample. The reader can then challenge Quark on novelty, Witness on encounters, or Atanor on parent cycles; other independent crossings remain available.

**BOG:CYCLE:HUMAN:RETURN** — one person's interpretation, observed difference, negative test and own next question return, with the person's authorship, to the shared research entry when *they choose*. No visitor questions are collected automatically. No account, payment, agreement or model invocation is required.

**BOG:CYCLE:HUMAN:CLOSE** — the human is free to exit. That freedom is part of the design, not a missing feature.
