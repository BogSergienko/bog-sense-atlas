What S05 actually showed

45 selected Vostok CO₂ points: an exploratory 23/41/100 kyr harmonic model modestly improved held-out RMSE against a mean baseline in two directions (32.13 vs 34.05 ppm; 28.94 vs 30.75 ppm). A 14-event igneous-province scan selected a 56.4 Ma peak, but a global null test found similar or stronger peaks in about 99.2% of random chronologies. This does not establish a universal geological clock.

The source article includes graphical displays, the rejected S04 reversal, calculation provenance and future directions. The figures depict computations and chosen historical subsets; they are not new physical observations.
