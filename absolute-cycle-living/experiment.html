01 / a missing origin

Two networks, one return

LCM(6, 8, 12) = LCM(3, 8) = 24

Change the child network without changing the common return. The inverse operation has 12 candidates in the predeclared catalog of distinct pairs and triples from integer periods 2 to 12.



02 / global harmony
Break the local rules, preserve an invariant
H = (x₀ − x₃, x₁ − x₄, x₂ − x₅) mod 3
All six coordinates are in {0,1,2}. Shift all coordinates by +1 mod 3: local fixed-coordinate conditions fail, but H remains unchanged. Shift the first three by +1 and the last three by +2: H changes. The full test enumerates exactly 3⁶ = 729 states.
Not executed
This is a mathematical counterexample to “every local rule must be preserved for a global relation to be preserved.” It is not a measurement of the universe.


03 / Earth-year offset
Move δ, keep the observation
xᵢ(t) = sin(2π(t − δ)/Tᵢ + φᵢ)
If initial phases are also free, δ can shift by k while φᵢ simultaneously shifts by +2πk/Tᵢ. Drag the candidate offset. Even a huge number of candidate years does not become a measured correction.
Three model periods: 23,000 / 41,000 / 100,000 years. Display only; no measured Earth-year drift.
